How could a Planning Framework make the greatest impact to community wellbeing?

Help identify which planning actions and outcomes you believe would most improve community wellbeing, prevent displacement, and support residents and businesses to remain and thrive in Little Jamaica. Community in Public is gathering community perspectives to assess how the City’s evolving Little Jamaica Planning Framework meets community priorities and Planning Frameworks prepared for other neighbourhoods. You can review Development Review - midtown team's working draft vision in Step 3 of this survey. Further developed documents from the City will be added once provided by the City.

Cheryll Case, Founder and Executive Director of Community in Public is a member of Development Review's Stakeholder Advisory Committee for the Little Jamaica Planning Framework. This survey is conducted this survey independently to inform Cheryll and Community in Public's advice provided to the City as an SAC member. The survey and assessments are not official City of Toronto or Stakeholder Advisory Committee engagement activities.
