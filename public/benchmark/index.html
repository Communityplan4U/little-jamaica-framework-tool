OUR METHODOLOGY

How the leaderboard works

  1. Each assessed local study contributes its existing relative score to each councillor who represented the area during the documented study period.
  2. For multiple studies, start with 66% for the most recent and divide the remaining 34% evenly among older studies. A single study starts at 100%. If the latest dates are tied, split the 66% equally; if all dates tie, start with equal weights.
  3. Divide each study’s starting weight by the number of study-period councillors associated with it.
  4. Normalize the adjusted weights to total 100%, then multiply each original study score by its final weight and sum the contributions. Ranking uses unrounded results; ties share the same rank.

Final study weight = (starting weight ÷ associated councillor count) ÷ sum of those adjusted weights within the portfolio.

Interpretation. Shared representation is a proxy, not proof of individual responsibility or participation. Councillors with one assessed study retain its full score after normalization. Citywide bodies without local councillor assignments are excluded from councillor rankings. Search hides rows, never changes their scores or ranks. The Framework and TOR scales differ; they are not combined.

Recency. Based on assessed document dates where established, otherwise documented study or committee periods. The Little Jamaica and Weston TOR issue dates have not been confirmed; the current ordering provisionally treats the 2026 Little Jamaica TOR as later than Weston’s October 2025 LAC start. The Little Jamaica framework vision is ordered after the February 2026 framework. No exact issue date is implied by these ordering choices.

Scope. These scores assess written provisions, not individual councillor or staff performance, document implementation, or overall planning quality. Earlier study-period councillors are not presented as current officeholders. The rankings are not a complete record of any councillor’s work.
